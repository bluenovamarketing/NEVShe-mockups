NEVShe chapter overview

The Nevada Society of Healthcare Engineers was officially recognized as ASHE’s newest chapter at the Healthcare Facilities Innovation Conference in Minneapolis on August 2, 2026.

A chapter for Nevada

The recognition establishes Nevada’s first official ASHE chapter. NEVShe joins Region 9 alongside California and Arizona.

The recognition photograph

NEVShe President Ricardo Barrera, Secretary Rich Park, and Director at Large Ismael Lopez-Ferratt are pictured with Narsimha Irrinki, ASHE Region 9 President.
