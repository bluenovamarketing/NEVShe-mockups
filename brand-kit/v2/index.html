01 · Proposed premise

Statewide by design.

NEVShe should feel like a credible professional chapter with real momentum—not a tourism brand, a generic medical association, or a Las Vegas-only organization.

Working idea: deep chapter blue provides authority; violet adds forward energy; silver linework references Nevada and the built environment; expansive photography connects people, place, and infrastructure.

Editorial confidence, everyday clarity.

BODY + UI · MANROPE 400–700

Clear enough for a technician on a phone. Polished enough for an executive briefing.

Use generous spacing, sentence case, a 16px minimum body size, and strong visible states for every action.

Preferred light presentation

Keep clear space at least equal to the cap height of the “N.” Do not crop, skew, redraw, recolor, or separate the mark and wordmark.

05 · Photography + AI direction

Nevada is the setting. Facilities work is the story.

Concept image · AI-generated for direction only

North, south, urban, mountain, desert, rural.

Replace with rights-cleared commissioned photography where available; verify every depicted facility, location, person, system, and safety practice before publication.

People + practiceEngineers, technicians, construction teams, educators, inspections, collaboration.
Systems + safetyHVAC, electrical, water, life safety, resilient utilities, facilities operations.
Statewide placesReno/Sparks, Carson City, Tahoe, Las Vegas/Henderson, Elko and rural Nevada.
Balance ruleAny set of 3+ images includes a rural healthcare environment and both north and south.
Landscape roleMountains, water and high desert provide context; they do not replace the profession.
AvoidCasinos, nightlife, Strip dominance, generic clinical scenes, unsafe or implausible systems.
Mandatory editorial image policy

Fresh imagery on every page.

0×The same image may never repeat on one page.
3× maxAny image may appear no more than three times across all core and core-service pages.

A new crop does not create a new image. Production will use an image ledger tracking source asset, page, section, crop, location/subject, rights, and total uses. Logos, functional icons, and decorative linework are excluded.

09 · Motion + behavior

Extravagant, friendly, and alive.

Layered atmosphere

Gradient drift, light, parallax, and oversized linework create cinematic depth.

Connected statewide story

Silver traces, image transitions, timelines, counters, and marquees guide exploration.

Hover / focus lift

Friendly interaction

Buttons sweep, cards lift and glow, filters respond, and menus reveal in roughly 180–350ms.

Motion system: richer animation is a project requirement and will live in global Elementor templates/classes—not individual content entries. Reduced motion: remove looping movement, parallax, auto-marquees, and decorative hero geometry; preserve clear instant state changes and all content.
